ENTRY NO. 002 Week Two

Entering a Different World

Last week closed out with some good news — a new opportunity I've been building toward. But that's not really what this entry is about. It's about something bigger: what it actually feels like to walk out of one world, the only one you've ever known, and into a completely different one.

Being an athlete can make you an introvert to everyone who isn't one. You live inside a totally different rhythm for most of your life — different rooms, different pressure, different people — and when that ends, the outside world can feel like it's moving at a different speed. For me, that transition has actually been going well. My mental state is in a better place without football than it was with it, and honestly, I think I knew that the moment my last NFL game ended.

What's helped most is accepting things — the good, the bad, and whatever the outcome turns out to be. I've lived by one line for a long time: if you can look in your own mirror and honestly say you gave everything you had, there's no room left for regret. Showing up and executing, every single day, is what clears that out.

— DJ
